Status Edit Android

Foreground service demonstrations for Google Play review. The recordings use synthetic demonstration videos. No reviewer account or sign-in is required to view them.

Video preparation and upload

The user taps “Send to WhatsApp”. Status Edit prepares the selected clip and uploads the result. Progress and a Stop control are visible. When recipient confirmation is needed, the interface shows that state; after acceptance it updates to “Sent to WhatsApp”. The recording omits the wait and the external messaging screen.

On-device vocal separation

The user taps “Separate vocals”. Processing progress and a Stop control are visible. The later portion shows the user stopping the task and the editor returning to its idle state. The intervening waiting period has been shortened.

These are finite tasks explicitly started by the user. The Android service also displays an ongoing progress notification with a Stop action while active. Android 15 and newer use the mediaProcessing service type for local media processing; Android 13–14 use dataSync. Network transfers use dataSync.
