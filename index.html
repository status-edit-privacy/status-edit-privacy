Status Edit Prepare. Sync. Share. English עברית IPHONE VIDEO WORKFLOW Get the clip ready. Keep the moment. Status Edit is an iPhone app for preparing video and audio quickly: choose a clip, sync external audio, refine the sound, preview the result, export, and—when you choose—send the finished MP4 through the official Status Edit WhatsApp delivery flow. Fast video preparation External-audio sync On-device AI vocal balance WhatsApp delivery Operated by Yehuda Hershenbaum Sync Match a selected video to a longer external recording and keep the edit aligned without manually hunting through the full recording. Shape the sound Preview and adjust the audio workflow, including on-device vocal/music separation where you choose to use it. Send Export the final MP4, save it to Photos, use the iOS Share Sheet, or send it through the Status Edit WhatsApp delivery service. Privacy Policy Summary Last updated: September 6, 2026 Most editing work happens on your device. Status Edit does not require a user account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Network processing is used when you explicitly connect or use the WhatsApp delivery feature. Media processing Video/audio editing, synchronization, previews, local temporary files, and AI vocal separation are processed on the device. The AI vocal-separation model is bundled with Status Edit, and media analyzed for vocal separation is not sent to a model-hosting provider. Important change: if you choose Send to WhatsApp, the finished exported MP4 is uploaded to Status Edit delivery infrastructure so it can be delivered through WhatsApp. This is the only normal Status Edit workflow in which the finished media is intentionally uploaded to Status Edit server infrastructure. WhatsApp connection and delivery To connect WhatsApp, Status Edit creates a device-specific installation identifier and receives a device token from the Status Edit backend. When you send the one-time CONNECT message from WhatsApp, the backend associates that token with the WhatsApp phone number that sent the message. For delivery, the final MP4 is uploaded through Status Edit's backend and object-storage infrastructure, then sent using Twilio and the WhatsApp Business Platform. Cloudflare is used for backend hosting/storage, Twilio is used for messaging delivery, and Meta/WhatsApp operates the WhatsApp platform. These services may process information required to perform delivery, including the WhatsApp phone number, message/delivery metadata, the media or media URL needed for delivery, IP/network information, and technical request logs. What Status Edit stores Status Edit does not maintain a traditional user account. To keep a WhatsApp connection working, the backend may store a device token, the linked WhatsApp phone number, connection timestamps, and recent inbound-message timing needed for WhatsApp delivery rules. Finished MP4 files uploaded for WhatsApp delivery are intended as temporary delivery files. They may remain in server-side object storage until removed by automated retention rules or administrative cleanup. They are not uploaded for advertising, profiling, or AI training. Photos, files and local storage The app accesses only the photos/videos or audio files you select, subject to iOS permissions. When you request it, Status Edit can save a finished export to your Photos library. The app may create temporary local files while importing, previewing, separating audio, synchronizing, or exporting. Data collection and use Status Edit does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not intentionally sell personal information or use personal media for targeted advertising or tracking. Backend data is used to provide connection, delivery, reliability, abuse prevention, and operational troubleshooting for the features you choose to use. Third-party services • Cloudflare — backend execution and temporary/object storage used by the WhatsApp delivery system. • Twilio — WhatsApp messaging transport and delivery infrastructure. • Meta / WhatsApp — WhatsApp Business Platform and end-user messaging service. • Apple / iOS Share Sheet — if you manually share to another app or service, that destination's privacy practices apply after you choose to share. Your choices and deletion You control Photos access through iOS Settings and can stop using the WhatsApp delivery feature at any time. Files saved to Photos or shared to another destination are managed through those destinations. WhatsApp connection data and temporary delivery media are handled under the Status Edit backend retention and operational policies. Business information Status Edit is operated by Yehuda Hershenbaum.
